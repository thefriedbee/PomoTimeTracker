Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Your activity records are stored on your device. FocoTaps does not retain user activity data on its servers.

Optional AI suggestions and summaries use DeepSeek’s third-party API. Relevant information is sent for processing when you request an AI response. DeepSeek’s handling of that information is separate from FocoTaps’ own retention practice.

About FocoTaps

FocoTaps is a time-tracking app for recording activities, focusing on tasks, reviewing your time, and planning goals. This policy explains how information is handled in the app and on this support website.

Information on your device

Your records and settings are stored locally, including:

  • Activities, groups, time records, and event notes
  • Day and week notes, goals, targets, focus plans, and achievements
  • App preferences and information used by features you enable

Charts and local calculations use your saved records. FocoTaps does not sell your activity data or use it for advertising.

Optional AI suggestions and summaries

We use DeepSeek’s API to generate AI suggestions and summaries. When you request an AI response, relevant information is sent through the FocoTaps service to DeepSeek for processing:

  • Coach and statistics requests: period details, activity and group names, totals, calculated metrics, comparison summaries, and summarized time patterns.
  • Records summaries: summary statistics and activity breakdowns, plus relevant day/week notes and selected event-note text. Event-note entries can include activity names, group names, and time labels.

These requests use feature-specific information rather than your complete raw event timeline. Activity labels and notes can contain personal information. You can use normal recording and review without requesting AI responses.

Requests to the FocoTaps service also include a device-generated identifier, language, membership status, and feature information. The identifier and usage counts are used to apply AI request limits.

Activity data retention and DeepSeek

FocoTaps does not retain user activity data on its servers. Activity information submitted for an AI request is processed on demand to return a response; FocoTaps does not keep it as an activity history or backup. This statement concerns activity content, separately from service information such as device identifiers and request-limit counts.

DeepSeek receives the relevant AI request content as our third-party API provider. DeepSeek’s processing and retention are governed by the terms applicable to its API service, separately from FocoTaps’ own no-retention practice. For provider information, see DeepSeek’s Open Platform Terms of Service and DeepSeek’s Privacy Policy.

Optional iCloud backup

If you enable iCloud backup on a supported iOS device, a backup of your records and supported settings is stored in your iCloud storage through Apple’s services. This is separate from FocoTaps’ servers. Backups can include notes, activities, goals, and selected preferences.

Automatic backup is optional. You can turn it off in FocoTaps Settings. Turning it off stops future automatic uploads and does not delete existing backup copies. Backup is a snapshot of your data and does not continuously synchronize edits between devices.

Calendar feeds and Screen Time

If you add a calendar subscription, FocoTaps stores its URL and retrieves calendar content directly from that service. Calendar content may be cached on your device. Your calendar provider receives those requests. Keep private subscription URLs confidential; removing a feed from FocoTaps does not delete the original calendar.

If you authorize Screen Time features, FocoTaps uses on-device usage information to suggest estimated time ranges. Raw Screen Time reports and selection tokens are not included in the AI request formats described above. Activities you save from suggestions become part of your records and can contribute to AI summaries you request. You can disable suggestions and manage authorization in device settings.

Apple services and connected devices

On supported devices, the paired Apple Watch app receives selected activity names, timer state, and daily totals. Notifications, widgets, and Live Activities may display selected information on device surfaces. You can manage their visibility through app and system settings.

Apple handles App Store payments. FocoTaps uses purchase and subscription information to determine membership access and restore eligible purchases.

Your choices and questions

No FocoTaps account is required for normal recording and review. You can edit or remove local records, choose not to use AI, remove calendar feeds, disable Screen Time suggestions, and turn off automatic iCloud backup. Copies in iCloud or elsewhere must be managed separately from your local records.

For privacy questions or requests about information handled by FocoTaps, email pomotimetracker@outlook.com. We receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send when contacting support.
