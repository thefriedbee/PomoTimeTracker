Privacy Policy — PomoTimeTracker

Last updated: April 2026

Who we are

FocoTaps/PomoTimeTracker is a time-tracking app that helps you log activities and use the Pomodoro technique. This policy describes how we handle your information.

Data we collect

By default, the information you create in the app stays on your device, including:

  • Time-tracking events (activities, start and end times)
  • Activity names and groups you set up
  • App settings and preferences

We do not maintain a copy of your app data on our servers. We do not use your information to build profiles or for advertising.

Advanced statistics (optional)

If you choose features that use advanced statistics, the app may send the minimum data needed to our servers so we can generate those statistics and insights for you. Processing happens on demand when you request it: we compute the result for that request and do not retain your app information or your tracking history on our systems afterward. Your device remains where your complete records and settings are kept; the server is used only for those requested calculations, not as a backup or archive of your data.

Where your data is stored

Your ongoing records and settings are stored on your device (for example in local storage). Aside from the optional advanced statistics flow described above, we do not have access to your data and do not send it to third parties for analytics or marketing.

Third parties

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for their own purposes. The app may use system features (such as local notifications for Pomodoro timers) that are handled entirely on your device. Any network use tied to advanced statistics is limited to our service for that on-demand processing.

No account required

PomoTimeTracker does not require an account for normal use. There is no sign-in, and we do not keep a persistent archive of your app data on our servers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or the app, contact us at pomotimetracker@outlook.com.
