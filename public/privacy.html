Privacy Policy — PomoTimeTracker

Last updated: February 2025

Who we are

PomoTimeTracker is a time-tracking app that helps you log activities and use the Pomodoro technique. This policy describes how we handle your information.

Data we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store your data on our servers. All information you create in the app stays on your device, including:

  • Time-tracking events (activities, start and end times)
  • Activity names and groups you set up
  • App settings and preferences

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored only on your device (e.g. in local storage). We do not have access to it and do not send it to any third party.

Third parties

We do not share your data with third parties. The app may use system features (such as local notifications for Pomodoro timers) that are handled entirely on your device.

No account required

PomoTimeTracker does not require an account. There is no sign-in, and we do not collect or store your data on our servers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or the app, contact us at pomotimetracker@outlook.com.
